This app is essentially a **Digital Wellbeing app for skills instead of apps**.

# Hours — Skill Time Tracking App

### Core Idea

People often want to learn skills like DSA, Piano, Coding, YouTube Content Creation, Reading, etc., but they don't know where their time is actually going.

Hours helps users **track, visualize, and improve the time they spend on their skills**.
- https://hours.debo.life/
---

# How It Works

## 1. Create Skills

Users can create skills they want to work on:

* DSA
* Piano
* YouTube Scripting
* Gym
* Reading
* Any custom skill

---

## 2. Set Learning Goals

For each skill, users can choose a target:

### Option 1

Spend **X hours per day**

Example:

* DSA → 2 hours/day

### Option 2

Practice **X times per week**

Example:

* Piano → 3 times/week

### Option 3

Spend **X hours per week**

Example:

* Reading → 12 hours/week

---

## 3. Start a Session

Every skill has a **Start Button**.

When clicked:

* Timer starts
* Time is automatically recorded
* Remaining target time is shown

Example:

Goal = 2 hours DSA

After 1 minute:

* Completed = 1 min
* Remaining = 1 hr 59 min

Very simple and distraction-free UI.

---

## 4. Motivation During Sessions

Show small motivational messages while users work.

Examples:

### At Start

* "Starting is the hardest step."
* "One session at a time."
* "Let's build momentum."

### Near Completion (90%)

* "You're almost there!"
* "Just a little more."

### Goal Completed

* "Well done, Champ!"
* "Today's target completed."

Messages can be randomized.

---

## 5. Built-in Pomodoro

Users can optionally enable:

* 25 min work + 5 min break
* 50 min work + 10 min break

during skill sessions.

---

## 6. Daily Timeline

Every day gets its own log page.

Users can see:

* What they worked on
* When they started
* When they stopped
* Total time spent

Example:

9:00 AM – 10:30 AM → DSA
11:00 AM – 12:00 PM → Piano
4:00 PM – 5:00 PM → YouTube

---

## 7. Manual Time Entry

Sometimes users forget to start the timer.

They should be able to add records manually.

Example:

"I practiced Piano from 7 PM to 8 PM."

The app will add that hour to their statistics.

---

## 8. Time Visualization

Show daily activity similar to Digital Wellbeing.

Instead of:

Instagram → 2h
YouTube → 1h

Show:

DSA → 2h
Piano → 1h
Reading → 45m

Using:

* Pie charts
* Bars
* Timeline view

---

## 9. Calendar View

Users can click any day on a calendar.

The app shows:

* Skills worked on
* Exact timeline
* Total productive hours
* Goal completion status

This creates a personal history of learning.
